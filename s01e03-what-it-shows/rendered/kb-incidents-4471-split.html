The problems

parser: day-first dates read month first

The shared parser reads every date month first. Every tenant on file writes the day first, so a booking for the seventh of April goes out for the fourth of July. This is the bug #4471 describes, and it hits every tenant through the app, email and the booking-v2 form.

Fix: parse dates in the tenant’s locale (dayFirst() in causes.ts already asks the platform).

hint: booking-v3 tells people to type month first

The booking-v3 form prints a month-first hint under its date field, then reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. People type what the hint says, and the date comes out flipped. Only tenants on booking-v3 have it, and only through the form, so the parser fix does not touch it.

Fix: print the tenant’s own order under the field, as booking-v2 does.

One-day move: dates move by one day, no swap

These reports describe a date that moved by one day: an invite that says Tuesday while the calendar says Monday, every booking in one month one day off, a client in Lisbon who got Tuesday while a client in Toronto got Monday, an invite that arrived before the booking was made. The desk had already listed them in desk-notes.json.

Likely cause, not yet confirmed: a date converted through UTC or the viewer’s time zone, so it lands on the day before. Engineering should check how invite dates are stored and rendered.
